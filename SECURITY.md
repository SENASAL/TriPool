# Security Policy

SENASAL develops cybersecurity and blockchain project concepts. Software and program contracts described as being in design or viability assessment have no public release. We take the security of our code, contracts and users seriously, and we welcome responsible reports.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue for security problems.**

Report privately by either of these routes:

1. **GitHub private reporting:** if enabled for the affected repository, use the **"Report a vulnerability"** button in its *Security* tab. If unavailable, use the email address below.
2. **Email:** `c.senasal@gmail.com`

Please include:

- A clear description of the issue and its impact.
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept.
- The affected repository, contract address or token identifier, and version or commit.
- Your contact details, if you want us to credit you.

## What to expect

- We aim to **acknowledge** your report within **5 business days**.
- We aim to share an **initial assessment** within **15 business days**.
- We will keep you informed of progress and tell you when the issue is fixed.
- With your permission, we will credit you once the fix is public.

These are targets, not guarantees. Complex issues may take longer.

## Scope

In scope:

- Code in repositories under [github.com/SENASAL](https://github.com/SENASAL).
- SENASAL-issued tokens: CSDT and RCVC (Waves), NAL, CYAI, TBL and DPS (MultiversX).
- Any SENASAL smart contracts explicitly published as deployed and in scope. Design documents do not imply that contracts have been deployed.
- Our websites under `senasal.com`.

Out of scope:

- Third-party platforms and services (exchanges, wallets, explorers, blockchains).
- Social engineering, phishing or physical attacks against our team.
- Denial-of-service or spam against live systems.
- Reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.

## Rules for testing

- Test only on your own accounts and devices, or on test networks.
- Do not access, change or delete other people's data or funds.
- Do not run tests that degrade the service for others.
- Give us reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.

If you follow these rules and act in good faith, we will not take legal action against you for your research. This policy does not authorize testing third-party systems or actions outside its scope.

## Rewards

We do not run a formal bug bounty at this time. We may offer a reward at our discretion for serious findings, and we will always credit you if you wish.

## Supported versions

Our projects are under development. For repositories containing software, security fixes target the latest maintained code on the default branch. This does not imply that any public release exists. Projects marked as "design phase" have no released versions yet.

## Staying safe

- **We will never ask for your seed phrase or private keys.** Anyone who does is not us.
- **Verify tokens by their official identifier.** The full list is in our [profile README](https://github.com/SENASAL).
- Be careful with look-alike websites, fake support accounts and unsolicited messages offering tokens or airdrops.

Thank you for helping keep SENASAL and its users safe.
